●●14.1 The scene

Tuesday. EURUSD. D1 is bullish: the last HL is at 1.0780, price is in the discount half of the 1.0780–1.0900 range. H4 has just made an HL and a BOS up. The macro picture: the ECB sounds more hawkish than the Fed. US CPI is out at 15:30 Sofia time. This is the story of the day, told hour by hour. Drag the clock.

●●14.3 The evening review

The day ends at 20:00. The analyst opens the journal and answers five questions. Not to praise or punish themselves, but to separate the process from the outcome (Chapter 5).

QuestionAnswer for today
Which scenario happened?A: Asia swept in London, CPI below expectations, move up in New York.
Was the setup visible in advance?Yes: D1/H4 bullish, discount, Asia with liquidity below, CPI as the catalyst.
Did I respect the buffers?Yes: nothing between 15:00 and 16:00.
What was luck?The direction of CPI. Had the number come in above expectations, scenario B would have won, and the plan covered it.
What will I do differently?Nothing. The first London zone (10:40) came before the MSS and I rightly skipped it. I log that as a good decision.
Mentor's note

A day you stayed on the sidelines and the market left without you is not a lost day. If your plan said "wait" and you waited, it is a day won for discipline.

What you take from Dossier 14

  • Morning: direction, levels, news, scenarios A/B/C.
  • Asia: just mark. London: wait for sweep and MSS. 15:00–16:00 on CPI days: nothing.
  • New York: the read comes after the settle, in the D1/H4 direction, targeting liquidity.
  • Evening: the process is judged separately from the outcome.

No single right answer

The same day, but CPI comes out at 0.5% (above the expected 0.3%). At 15:30 EURUSD drops 40 pips, breaks the low of the London sweep (1.0806) and closes below it on H1. D1 is still bullish, but H1 has made a CHoCH down.

Task: which morning scenario wins? What does the checklist say? What does the evening review look like in this version?

Sample answer
  1. Scenario B (or C) wins: CPI above expectations supports the dollar; London scenario A is invalidated on a close below 1.0806.
  2. The checklist: D1 bullish, but H1 against it → conflict → WAIT. The macro verdict for the day turns YELLOW: the data contradict the weaker-dollar thesis.
  3. If there was a position from London (after the MSS), the stop below 1.0806 was hit: −1R. That is a normal loss with the plan followed.
  4. Evening review: the process was followed; the outcome is bad because of news whose direction cannot be known. Nothing to fix in the process. Tomorrow: a fresh analysis from D1, no anchoring to yesterday's thesis.
