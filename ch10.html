From Read to Decision: the 6-Step Framework

Plans are worthless, but planning is everything. Dwight Eisenhower

After this chapter you will be able to

  • go through everything from Chapters 6–9 in the same order, every time;
  • score the quality of an analysis from 0 to 25;
  • fill in the pre-analysis checklist, including the question about your own state.
Read before the chapter

This is a framework for thinking, not a trade instruction. It helps you put your observations in order and see when the picture is clear and when it isn't. The decision whether and how to take part in the market is yours alone, and only after months of practice on demo (Chapter 34).

●●10.1 The six steps

If any step is missing, the picture is incomplete. Then the right answer is “no decision today”. On most days that is exactly the answer, and that is normal.

  1. Direction (bias). D1 → H4 → H1. Is the structure bullish or bearish? Do D1 and H4 agree? (Chapters 6 and 8)
  2. Macro verdict. Is the fundamental wind blowing the same way? Is there major news in the next 2 hours? Verdict: GREEN, YELLOW or RED. (Part III)
  3. Zone (POI). OB + FVG, OB after a sweep, a clean FVG, or a level + liquidity, in the right half of the range. (Chapter 7)
  4. Trigger. An MSS on M5 or an engulfing candle in the zone. You wait for the candle to close. (Chapters 3 and 6)
  5. Invalidation. Where is the scenario wrong? Below the sweep's low or below the OB, plus a buffer. It is set before everything else. (Chapters 9 and 11)
  6. Target. The next liquidity in the direction (BSL or SSL). If there isn't at least 3R to it, the picture isn't worth it. (Chapter 12)
Mentor's note

Six steps sounds like a lot. After a hundred repetitions they take six seconds. After a thousand you will notice the missing step before you've looked for it.

●●10.2 Scoring the analysis: /25

Five criteria (direction, macro, zone, trigger and time) get 0 to 5 points each. Invalidation and target are not scored: either they are there or there is no decision. The total says how strong the picture is. It is not a prediction of whether price will go there. It is a measure of whether your analysis has enough support.

ScoreGradeWhat it means for the analysis
20–25A+Every support is there. Rare: a few times a month on one pair.
15–19BA good picture with one weaker support. In the framework this book comes from, a B means half size.
10–14CToo many “maybes”. For watching and the journal only.
under 10DNo picture. Close the chart.

Extra points some analysts add to “Zone” or “Trigger”: a regular divergence on H1/H4 in the direction (+2), an SMT divergence (+1), a hidden divergence on a continuation (+1). They reinforce, but never replace, the main five.

Grade · класA+, B, C, D: the quality of the picture, not a guarantee of the outcome.

●10.3 The pre-analysis checklist

Pilots run a checklist before every flight, even though they have flown thousands of times. Not because they can't remember, but because memory is least reliable exactly when you are tired, tense or overconfident. The last line is the most important.

StudentWhy is my state in the checklist? The analysis is either good or it isn't.

MentorThe analysis is good as long as you do it calmly. An angry person sees a setup everywhere. A tired one misses the news at 15:30. You are the instrument. Check the instrument.

Debrief

What you take from Dossier 10

  • Six steps: direction → macro → zone → trigger → invalidation → target. A missing step = no decision.
  • The /25 score measures the quality of the analysis: A+ ≥ 20, B 15–19, under 15 watch only.
  • The checklist is run every time, including the question “how am I?”.
  • On most days the right answer is “no decision today”.

No single right answer

EURUSD, 16:10 Sofia time. D1 and H4 bullish. Macro: the ECB is more hawkish than the Fed; no news until tomorrow. There was CPI at 15:30; price fell to an H1 OB (1.0812–1.0818), swept below the London low (1.0810) to 1.0806 and on M5 closed above the last LH (1.0821). The next BSL is the previous daily high, 1.0880. You took two losses in the morning.

Task: go through the six steps and give a /25 score on the five criteria. What grade is it? What does the last line of the checklist say?

Sample answer
  1. Direction: D1 + H4 bullish → 5/5.
  2. Macro: a policy gap in the euro's favour, no news → green, 5/5. (CPI was 40 minutes ago; the 30-minute buffer has passed.)
  3. Zone: H1 OB after a sweep under London → 4–5/5.
  4. Trigger: M5 MSS above 1.0821 → 5/5, on a closed candle.
  5. Time: New York kill zone → 5/5.
  6. Invalidation: below 1.0806 plus a buffer, about 1.0803 → if the entry zone is around 1.0815, the risk is about 12 pips. Target 1.0880 → about 65 pips ≈ 5R.
  7. Score about 24/25: an A+ picture.
  8. But: two losses in the morning. If your rule is “no new decisions after the daily limit”, or you feel the urge to “win it back”, the last line of the checklist says “no”. A good picture will come again. The account may not.
