How to use the glossary

  • Every term from the cards in the chapter margins, gathered in one place: 499 terms.
  • Search in English or in Bulgarian. The number on the right leads to the chapter where the term first appears.
  • The CSV button downloads the whole glossary for Excel or Anki.

Flashcards

The English term is on the front. Tap the card to see the Bulgarian name and the definition. "I know it" removes it from the deck, "Once more" sends it to the back. Learned cards are stored in this browser.
