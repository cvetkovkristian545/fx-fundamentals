Privacy Notice

In short

  • You can read the whole book without an account. Your progress then stays only in your browser.
  • With an account we store your email and your progress in the book, so it follows you to any device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and all saved progress at any time, in one click.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Kristian Tsvetkov, Sofia, Bulgaria, author of FX Fundamentals. For any privacy question or request, write to cvetkovkristian545@gmail.com.

2. Reading without an account

Your ticks, quiz scores, highlights, journal and backtest logs are saved in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request data when you open a page, and keeps them in its server logs for security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts are served from this site itself, not from Google. Links to other websites (for example TradingView or Forex Factory) take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Legal basis: performing the service you asked for when you created the account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We send no newsletters. The only emails you may receive are ones you trigger yourself, such as a password reset.

4. Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Accounts and progress are handled by Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC), acting as our processor under Google's data processing terms.
  • Your book progress is stored in the European Union (Cloud Firestore, Europe multi-region).
  • Sign-in records (Firebase Authentication) are processed on Google's global infrastructure, which includes the USA. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Your data is protected by security rules: each account can read and write only its own record.

5. How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Open the person icon on any page → Delete account and data. This removes your saved progress and then your account. If deletion fails (for example, because you signed in long ago), sign in again and repeat, or email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw from the service at any time. Email us to use any of these rights; we reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian supervisory authority: Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД), 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592, www.cpdp.bg.

7. Browser storage and cookies

The site uses your browser's local storage, and after sign-in Firebase's storage, only to keep your progress and your session. This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so no consent banner is needed. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Age and changes

Accounts are for readers aged 14 or older. Younger readers can use the book without an account.
