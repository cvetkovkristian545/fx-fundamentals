The three examples

EURUSD, London, an A+ picture

A teaching example with invented but realistic numbers. Tuesday, 10:05 Sofia time. The Sunday bias file: EUR leaning down, ~56%, confidence 4. Today there is no red news for EUR and USD until 15:30.

StepRead
1. DirectionD1 bearish (lower highs for three weeks), H4 bearish, H1 has just made a lower high.
2. MacroGREEN: the bias file points the same way, no news until 15:30.
3. ZoneBearish H1 OB 1.1268–1.1274 with an FVG inside, above which lies the Asian high 1.1279 (liquidity).
4. Trigger10:20: price takes the Asian high up to 1.1283, makes an MSS down below 1.1270 on M5 and closes.
5. InvalidationAbove the sweep high: 1.1283 + a 1.5-pip buffer = 1.12845. Defined first.
6. TargetThe next liquidity: yesterday's low 1.1222. From an entry at 1.1269: 47 pips to the target, 15.5 to the stop, about 3R.
CriterionPointsWhy
Direction D1+H45D1 and H4 in one direction
Macro5Green, no news
Zone5OB + FVG after a sweep
Trigger5MSS on M5, closed candle
Time5London kill zone
Total25 / 25Grade A+

Verdict: the picture has every support. This is a description of the analysis, not an order to trade. If the trader takes part, the risk is 1%, and the size is calculated from 15.5 pips.

The lesson

A+ pictures are rare: a few times a month on one pair. If you see them every day, your criteria have gone soft.

GBPUSD, New York, a C picture

Wednesday, 16:40. In the morning UK CPI came in 0.1 pp above expectations. At 16:00 the BoE governor speaks. Bias: GBP up, ~56%, confidence 3.

StepRead
1. DirectionD1 bullish, H4 in a range for four days.
2. MacroYELLOW: the direction matches, but the 16:00 speech is still being digested.
3. ZonePrice is in the middle of the H4 range. No clean zone.
4. TriggerA bullish pin bar on M5, but no MSS.
5. InvalidationUnclear: below the pin bar (8 pips) or below the range low (35 pips).
6. TargetThe top of the range, about 2R from the near stop, under 1R from the far one.
CriterionPointsWhy
Direction D1+H43D1 clear, H4 unclear
Macro3Yellow
Zone0The middle of the range
Trigger3A pin bar without an MSS
Time3The end of the New York window
Total12 / 25Grade C

Verdict: for observation and the journal only. It is logged as "not a setup", with the reason: a zone in the middle of the range.

The lesson

A good bias does not rescue a bad location. Most days look like this, and the right answer is "no decision today".

EURUSD after CPI: a sweep created by news

A teaching example following the template from Chapter 13. Wednesday, 15:30, US CPI comes in below expectations. The spike drops EURUSD 25 pips below the London low, then price comes back. Bias: EUR up, ~55%, confidence 3.

StepRead
1. DirectionD1 bullish, H4 bullish.
2. MacroGREEN after the buffer: the soft CPI favours EUR; the next news is tomorrow.
3. ZoneA bullish M15 OB formed by the reversal after the spike; the spike low is below the London low.
4. Trigger16:05 (35 minutes after the number): MSS up on M5 and a return into the OB.
5. InvalidationBelow the spike low plus a buffer: an 18-pip stop.
6. TargetThe daily high from before CPI: 52 pips, about 2.9R.
CriterionPointsWhy
Direction D1+H45One direction
Macro5Green after the buffer
Zone3OB after a spike, less clean
Trigger5MSS after things calm down
Time3New York window, after news
Total21 / 25Grade B

Verdict: a good picture with one weaker support and a target just under 3R. Within the book's framework, a grade B halves the size, and a target under 3R is a reason to pass. It is logged, whatever the decision.

The lesson

A news spike creates liquidity, but also noise. The thirty minutes after the number and confirmation from D1/H4 are conditions, not decorations.

Exercise

Open TradingView, start Bar Replay on a random date and go through the six steps for EURUSD. Write tables like the ones above, then play the chart forward. Repeat ten times. How many are A+, B, C and D? If most are A+, the problem is in the scoring, not the market.
