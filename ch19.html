Reading the Economy and Forex Factory

It is difficult to make predictions, especially about the future. A Danish saying, often attributed to Niels Bohr

After this chapter you will be able to

  • explain what GDP, PMI, NFP, unemployment, JOLTS and retail sales measure;
  • set Forex Factory to Sofia time and to the currencies you follow;
  • read the Actual, Forecast, Previous row and say whether it is a surprise;
  • recognise a yield curve inversion and why people talk about it.

●19.1 How an economy is measured

The economy is huge and nobody sees all of it. So statisticians measure it in pieces, each with a different frequency and delay. A trader does not need to remember every number. They need to know what each number says and which number the market is watching right now.

Mentor's note

The market has a "favourite number" in every period. When it is fighting inflation, CPI weighs most. When it fears recession, NFP and unemployment move to the front. The question every week is: which number is the market afraid of right now?

Leading · водещAn indicator that changes before the economy does.
Lagging · изоставащAn indicator that confirms what has already happened.
Revision · ревизияA correction to a past number. With NFP it can matter more than the new one.

●19.2 Forex Factory step by step

Forex Factory (forexfactory.com) is the free calendar almost every trader opens in the morning. You set it up once, then look at it every day.

  1. Time zone. The time is at the top right. Click it and choose (GMT+02:00) Sofia or turn on automatic daylight saving. If the zone is wrong, everything in the calendar is off by an hour or two.
  2. Filter. The funnel icon above the calendar. Keep the currencies you follow (to start, USD and EUR, then GBP, JPY). Tick High and Medium impact.
  3. View. "This Week" on Sunday evening, "Today" every morning.
  4. The folders. Red = high impact, orange = medium, yellow = low, grey = holiday or a speech with no expected impact.
  5. Details. The icon on the right of each row opens a description: what it measures, when it comes out, Usual Effect (whether higher is good or bad for the currency), history with past values and a chart.
  6. News. The "News" tab collects headlines by currency. Useful to see what moved the market today.
Watch on Forex Factory

The colour of Actual is not about the direction of the number but about the currency. Green means the result is better for the currency than the forecast. Red means worse. For unemployment, lower is better, so unemployment below forecast will show green.

Impact · ефектHow strongly the event usually moves the currency.
Usual EffectThe rule in the description: whether a higher result is good or bad for the currency.

●●19.3 Actual, Forecast, Previous: the surprise moves price

Every row has three numbers. Previous is the past. Forecast is the analysts' consensus, already built into the price. Actual is the new one. The market reacts to the gap between Actual and Forecast, not to the number itself. A good number that is weaker than expected can push the currency down.

Myth

The number came out "good", so the currency will go up.

Reality

If the market was waiting for something even better, the currency can fall. On top of that, revisions to last month, the second number in the same report (core versus headline) and trader positioning can reverse the first move within minutes.

Consensus · консенсусThe analysts' average forecast.
Surprise · изненадаActual minus Forecast.
WhipsawA sharp move one way followed by a sharp move the other.

●●19.4 The yield curve and inversion

The state borrows for different terms: 3 months, 2 years, 10, 30. If you plot the yields by term, you get the yield curve. Normally the longer loan pays more, because risk and inflation over 10 years are greater. The curve slopes up.

When short-term yields are above long-term ones, the curve is inverted. It means the market expects the central bank to cut rates in the future, usually because a slowdown is coming. In the US the 2-year/10-year inversion has preceded most recessions since the 1970s, but with very different lags, and sometimes with no recession at all. It is a warning, not a clock.

Normal: growth and moderate inflation. Banks profit from the gap between short deposits and long loans.

Flat: a transition. The market is not sure whether a slowdown follows.

Inverted: the market expects rate cuts. Historically a signal of recession risk. When it turns back up ("re-steepening"), the recession has often already begun.

There is another kind of steepening: when long yields rise faster because the market is worried about debt and inflation. Then the curve gets steeper for the "bad" reason. Chapter 24 comes back to this.

Yield curve · крива на доходносттаBond yields by term.
Inversion · инверсияShort yields are above long ones.
2s10sThe gap between the 10-year and the 2-year yield.

●●●19.5 One week: calendar versus price

Here is a real week from the notes this book grew out of: 12–16 October 2026. The data are recorded from the notes of those days and have not been checked separately. What matters is not the pips but the logic.

The lesson

A whole week of hot data and the dollar fell. Why? The market had already priced a rate hike almost fully, and positioning was overloaded in one direction. When everyone has already bought, there is no one left to buy the good news. That is why in Chapter 28 we will ask not only "what are the data" but "what is already in the price and who is trapped".

StudentThen why watch the data at all if price does the opposite?

MentorBecause without the data you would not know price is doing the opposite. The divergence itself is the signal.

●●●Go deeperRevisions, seasonal adjustment and the size of a surprise▾

Most headline numbers are seasonally adjusted: the normal pattern of the month (for example hiring before Christmas) is removed so that months can be compared. Adjustments change, and so do earlier months. NFP revises the previous two months every release: a "beat" of 50,000 with a downward revision of 80,000 to earlier months is weak news.

How big a surprise is depends on how much economists disagree. The forecast on Forex Factory is a median. If forecasts range from 100,000 to 250,000, a result of 230,000 is normal. If they range from 170,000 to 190,000, the same 230,000 is a shock. Some terminals show the surprise in standard deviations of the forecasts for exactly this reason.

Read three things, in this order: the headline against the forecast, the revisions, and the detail that the central bank cares about most (for NFP: wages and the unemployment rate).

Debrief

What you take from Dossier 19

  • Each number measures a piece of the economy: PMI leads, GDP confirms, NFP and CPI move the most.
  • Forex Factory: Sofia time zone, filter by currency and impact, details with Usual Effect.
  • The colour of Actual is about the currency: green is better than forecast, red is worse.
  • The surprise versus forecast moves price, and revisions and the second number can reverse it.
  • An inversion is a recession warning, not a clock.
  • When hot data do not lift the currency, the market is telling you something about positioning.

First Friday of the month, 15:30. NFP: Actual 250K, Forecast 180K. Last month is revised from 150K to 70K. Unemployment Rate: 4.3% versus a 4.1% forecast. Average Hourly Earnings: 0.2% versus 0.3%. EURUSD first drops 30 pips, then within 5 minutes comes back above the pre-news level.

Task: read the four numbers. Which is "good" for USD and which "bad"? Why did the first move reverse? What is the overall takeaway for the Fed? What do you do as a trader over the next hour?

Sample answer
  1. NFP above forecast: green for USD. Revision down by 80K: bad, it fully eats the 70K surprise. Unemployment above forecast: red. Wages below forecast: red, less inflationary pressure.
  2. The first move reacts to the headline number. Then the market reads the revision and the other three numbers and reverses. On net, three of the four signals are weak for USD.
  3. Takeaway: the labour market is weaker than it looks; a more dovish Fed is more likely. That is bullish for EURUSD, but it is one picture, not a certainty.
  4. Action: the NFP protocol (−30 / +60 minutes with no new positions). After that: you wait for structure on M5/M15 in the direction of the new wind and a zone, as in Chapter 10. You do not chase the first reversal.
