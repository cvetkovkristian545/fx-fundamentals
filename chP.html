What is here

  • Random exercises from the scenarios in the book: liquidity, structure, levels, sessions, management.
  • Every scenario can also appear mirrored, bullish or bearish, and the answers are shuffled.
  • Your score and best streak are stored in this browser.

Exercise

Tap the line or the button. Then "Next". Ten correct answers in a row mean you read the pictures confidently. They don't mean you are ready to trade live: that is what the journal is for.

Deliberate practice · целенасочена тренировкаShort repetitions of one skill with fast feedback.

●●P.2 Game: Find the setup

Ten charts, 20 seconds each. Click the candle that answers the question. A correct answer earns 10 points plus the remaining seconds, so the maximum is 300. The charts are generated at random, so every game is different.

Press "Start" when you are ready.
