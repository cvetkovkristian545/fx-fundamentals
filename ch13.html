News Risk Protocol

The market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent. Attributed to John Maynard Keynes

After this chapter you will be able to

  • split news into red, orange and yellow, and know the buffer around each;
  • check Forex Factory for 30 seconds before every session;
  • recognise a sweep created by news and know when (and whether) to read it.

●13.1 Why news is a separate world

In a normal hour EURUSD travels 10–20 pips. In the first minute after a surprise CPI it can travel 40 one way and 30 the other. The spread widens, stops fill with slippage, and candles lose their usual meaning. That is why news gets its own rules. Their goal is not for you to profit from the news but for the news not to catch you by surprise.

●●13.4 A sweep created by news

Sometimes the news creates a setup by itself. The spike after the number often runs past an important level with liquidity (the London low, PDH), takes the stops and comes back. Once the market calms down, an MSS in the opposite direction forms on the lower timeframe, along with a zone (OB or FVG) around the spike.

Requirements to read it as a setup: at least 30 minutes after the news, D1/H4 structure confirms the direction, and there is at least 3R to the next liquidity. Without them it is just noise.

News spike · новинарски шипThe sharp move in the first seconds and minutes after the release.
Settle · успокояванеVolatility returning to normal, usually 15–30 minutes after the number.

●●13.5 Geopolitical shock

Wars, attacks and escalations are not in the calendar. When they happen, the market switches to risk-off mode (Chapter 22). The typical reaction:

Swiss francUpSafe haven
AUD, NZD, emerging-market currenciesDownRisk currencies
Stock indicesDownRisk-off

First steps: check whether you hold several positions that are really the same bet (for example long AUDUSD and long NZDUSD). Do not open new risk positions until the market finds structure.

Myth

If I know what the news will be, I know where price will go.

Reality

Price reacts to the gap between the number and the expectation, not to the number itself (Chapter 19). Good news that everyone expects can push price down.

●●●Go deeperThe second before the number▾

In the last seconds before a big release, banks and market makers pull their quotes or widen them sharply: nobody wants to be on the wrong side of the number. The order book becomes thin exactly when the most orders arrive.

A stop order is not a price guarantee. When it is triggered it becomes a market order and takes whatever price is available. In a thin book that can be 10, 20 or more pips away. Slippage is also asymmetric: in a fast move against you, you get the worse price; a limit order in your favour simply may not be filled.

That is why the protocol in this chapter is built around time, not price: be out or protected before the number, and come back after the first minutes, when quotes return and the spread normalises.

Debrief

What you take from Dossier 13

  • Red folder: no new positions inside the buffer (CPI ±30, NFP −30/+60, central banks ±60 min).
  • Forex Factory for the next 4 hours before every analysis.
  • A spike can create a sweep. Read it only after it settles, with an MSS and in the D1/H4 direction.
  • Geopolitical shock: gold, yen and franc up; AUD, NZD and indices down.

No single right answer

Wednesday. FOMC is at 21:00. Until 18:00 EURUSD sits in a tight range. At 21:00 price jumps 35 pips up above PDH, at 21:04 it drops back 50 pips, at 21:30 the press conference starts. D1 is bearish. A friend asks: "The spike above PDH is a sweep, right? Should I sell now?"

Task: what would you tell them? What are the buffers here? What conditions must be met for there to be a setup, and when at the earliest?

Sample answer
  1. Yes, in shape it looks like a sweep of BSL above PDH in the direction of a bearish D1.
  2. But: the FOMC buffer runs from 20:00 to 22:00, and the press conference (21:30) is a separate event that matters for its whole duration. The market can flip everything twice more.
  3. Conditions: the end of the press conference, at least 30 minutes of settling, an MSS down on M5, a clear zone around the spike, 3R to the next SSL.
  4. At the earliest after about 22:30, when it is already New York afternoon and liquidity is falling. The honest answer is: "Not today. Write it in the journal and see tomorrow in London what is left."
