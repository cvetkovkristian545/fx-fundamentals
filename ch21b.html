Intermarket in Depth

Correlations are like umbrellas: they work until it really rains. Trader's wisdom

After this chapter you will be able to

  • read a correlation coefficient and know why it changes over time;
  • link a currency pair to the yield spread that really drives it;
  • use oil, metals, gold, stocks and the VIX as context for a currency;
  • spot a broken correlation and treat it as information;
  • run a five-minute intermarket check before the London open.

●●23.1 Correlation is a number that moves

Chapter 22 gave the map of links. This chapter measures them. The correlation coefficient goes from −1 to +1: +1 means two markets move together every day, −1 means always opposite, 0 means no linear link. It is measured over a window: the last 20, 60 or 120 days.

  1. It is calculated on changes (daily returns), not on price levels. Two lines that both rise for a year can have a correlation of zero day to day.
  2. It is not stable. Even if the "true" link were constant, the measured one would wobble because of noise. In real markets the true link changes too.
  3. It does not say who leads. Oil and CAD may both react to a third thing, like US growth.

Try it: noise in a constant link

The simulator generates two random series with the true correlation you choose and shows the correlation measured over a rolling window. Press "New data" several times.

With a 20-day window a true link of 0.6 regularly shows anywhere from about 0.2 to 0.9. That is why one week of "correlation broke" is usually noise, and why longer windows are steadier but slower.

Correlation coefficient · коефициент на корелацияA number from −1 to +1 for how closely two series move together.
Rolling window · плъзгащ се прозорецA fixed number of recent days used for each calculation.

●●23.2 Yield spreads: the engine under the pair

Money flows towards the higher expected return. That is why the most useful intermarket chart for a currency pair is usually not another currency, but the difference between the two countries' yields.

PairSpread to watchWhy that maturity
EURUSDGermany 2y − US 2yThe 2-year yield reflects expected central bank policy over the next two years
USDJPYUS 10y − Japan 10y (and the US 10y alone)Japanese investors compare long yields; JPY is very sensitive to US long rates
GBPUSDUK 2y − US 2yPolicy expectations, as long as fiscal trust holds
AUDUSDAustralia 2y − US 2y, plus ChinaRates matter, but commodities and China often dominate

On TradingView you can type a spread directly as a formula, for example TVC:DE02Y-TVC:US02Y, and put it under the EURUSD chart. When the two lines diverge for weeks, one of them is usually wrong, and it is more often the currency.

Mentor's note

On data days the bond market often moves first. If US 2-year yields jump 10 basis points in a minute after CPI, the dollar usually follows within minutes. Watching the yield is like seeing the lightning before the thunder.

Yield spread · спред на доходноститеThe difference between two yields, usually in basis points.
Basis point · базисна точкаOne hundredth of a percentage point: 0.01%.

Gold and real yields

Gold pays nothing. Its cost is the real yield you give up: the yield on inflation-protected US bonds (TIPS). For years gold moved opposite to real yields. Since 2022 that link has weakened, because central banks have bought record amounts of gold to reduce their dependence on the dollar. Rising real yields and rising gold together tell you a story about trust in the system, not about rates (Chapter 24).

TIPSUS Treasury bonds protected against inflation; their yield is a real yield.

●●23.4 Stocks, the VIX and the yen: the risk barometer

The VIX measures the implied volatility of S&P 500 options over the next 30 days: the market's "fear gauge". It is not a forecast of direction. It is the price of insurance.

VIX about 12–16

Calm. Carry trades work, risk currencies are bought, JPY and CHF are sold.

VIX about 20–30

Nervous. Correlations tighten, ranges widen. Cut size.

VIX above 30

Stress. Havens rise, AUDJPY falls, spreads widen.

VIX spike then fall

Often the end of a panic phase, as in August 2024.

For a currency trader the cleanest risk barometer is AUDJPY: a risk currency against a haven. When the S&P 500 falls and AUDJPY falls with it, the move is risk-off. When stocks fall but AUDJPY holds, the stock move is probably local, not global.

VIXThe index of expected 30-day volatility of the S&P 500, built from option prices.

●●●23.5 When correlations break

A correlation breaking is not an error. It is news: a sign that the main driver has changed. Three examples from recent history:

PeriodNormal linkWhat happenedWhat it meant
March 2020Gold up in a panicGold fell more than 10% at firstA dash for cash: everything is sold to get dollars
2022Bonds rise when stocks fallStocks and bonds fell togetherInflation, not growth, was the main risk; the "60/40" hedge failed
2022–2024Gold opposite to real yieldsGold rose despite higher real yieldsCentral bank buying and trust in the system took over
Sept 2022 (UK)Higher yields support the currencyGilt yields up, pound downA loss of fiscal trust (Chapter 29)
Two rules

1) Do not trade a correlation you have not checked in the last month. 2) If two positions are on highly correlated pairs (for example long EURUSD and long GBPUSD), you do not have two trades. You have one trade with double the risk.

Correlation breakdown · срив на корелациятаWhen a usual link stops working, often because the main driver changed.

●23.6 The five-minute intermarket check

Before the London open, look at six charts and write one line. The goal is not a prediction. It is to know which "weather" you are trading in.

#ChartQuestion
1US 2-year yieldIs the market pricing a more hawkish or more dovish Fed than yesterday?
2DXYIs the dollar broadly stronger or weaker?
3S&P 500 futures and VIXRisk-on, risk-off or calm?
4OilAny big move for CAD, inflation expectations or JPY?
5GoldFear, rates, or trust in the system?
6AUDJPYDoes the FX risk barometer agree with stocks?

Write it as one sentence in the journal: "Yields up, dollar up, stocks calm, oil flat: a rates day, not a risk day." In a month you will see which "weather" your setups work best in.

Debrief

What you take from Dossier 23

  • Correlation is measured on daily changes, over a window, and it moves even when nothing changes.
  • The 2-year spread is the engine under most pairs; for USDJPY, watch the 10-year.
  • Oil for CAD, metals and China for AUD, dairy for NZD, real yields and trust for gold.
  • VIX and AUDJPY are the risk barometer.
  • A broken correlation is news: the driver changed.
  • Two correlated positions are one position with double the risk.

No single right answer

For two weeks EURUSD has risen while the DE02Y−US02Y spread has fallen by 25 basis points in favour of the dollar. Stocks are calm, VIX is 13, oil is flat. Retail traders are 65% short EURUSD. You hold a long EURUSD and want to add a long GBPUSD.

Task: what does the divergence between EURUSD and the spread tell you? Which explanations are possible? What does adding GBPUSD do to your risk?

Sample answer
  1. The rate engine points down for EURUSD, but price is going up. One of the two is wrong, and more often it is the currency.
  2. Possible explanations: flows not related to rates (for example large asset purchases into Europe), short covering (the crowd is short), or the market looking past the next two years. Without one of these, the gap is a warning.
  3. EURUSD and GBPUSD are usually highly correlated. Adding GBPUSD roughly doubles the same dollar bet instead of adding a new idea. The decision is mine, but the risk should be counted as one larger position.
