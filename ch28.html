●31.2 The 30-minute setup

  1. Time zone. In the bottom right corner of the chart choose "(UTC+2/+3) Athens" or another zone with the same time as Sofia. All times in the book are Sofia time.
  2. A clean chart. Japanese candles, green and red, no grid. Remove everything you don't use.
  3. Sessions. Add a session indicator (search for "Sessions") and colour Asia, London and New York. Chapter 9 explained why.
  4. The list. Start small: DXY, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, XAUUSD and US 10Y. More pairs means less attention for each.
  5. Drawing tools. Horizontal line, rectangle for zones, the "Long/Short position" tool for risk. Save your favourites to the toolbar.
  6. Timeframes. Save quick buttons for D1, H4, H1, M15, M5.
  7. Alerts. Put an alert on every zone you are waiting for. That way you don't watch the chart all day.
  8. Save the layout. One name for analysis (D1/H4), one for execution (M15/M5).
The alert is your assistant

The most common beginner mistake is sitting in front of the chart and waiting. Sitting leads to boredom, boredom leads to trades without a setup. Draw the zones in the morning, set alerts and walk away. If price comes, your phone will call you.

Paper trading · хартиена търговияTrades with play money in TradingView. Useful for practice, but it does not replace a demo with a broker.

●31.3 Forex Factory: the calendar

Chapter 19 showed how to read the calendar. Here is the setup. Signing up at forexfactory.com is free and saves your settings.

  1. At the top right of the calendar choose a time zone that matches Sofia. Check: US data should come out at 15:30.
  2. Press the filter. Keep only red (high impact) and orange (medium impact) events.
  3. Keep only the currencies you trade. For most beginners: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY.
  4. Save the filter. On Sunday look at the week, in the morning look at the day.

Besides Forex Factory you will need a few more places. The table of news sources is in Chapter 28. For crypto add CoinMarketCap (prices, market cap, the fear and greed index) and CoinGlass (liquidations and leveraged positions).

Impact · ефектHow strongly the event usually moves the market. On Forex Factory: yellow, orange, red.

No single right answer

A beginner has a budget of about 75 euros a month for everything trading-related. They want TradingView Premium "to see more", a paid indicator for 40 dollars a month and a signal group subscription. They work from 9 to 18 and have free mornings only on Saturday and Sunday.

Task: which of these do they need now? When can they trade London at all? How would they split the budget and the time over the first three months?

Sample answer
  1. None of the three. Premium solves a problem they do not have yet. The paid indicator and the signals replace their own thinking, and that is exactly the skill they need to build.
  2. London opens at 10:00 Sofia time, when they are at work. After 18:00 New York fades and, by the rule from Chapter 9, no new positions are opened. The realistic path is swing with analysis in the evening (Chapter 30), and the weekends are for preparation, learning and backtesting. The market is closed on Saturday, but the charts are not.
  3. Budget: free TradingView and Forex Factory. The money is saved for a later prop attempt or for a small live account once the criteria from Chapter 34 are met.
  4. Time: Sunday for the bias file and backtesting, weekday evenings of 30–45 minutes for review and demo practice. Scalping London becomes realistic if the work schedule changes. The decision is theirs, but the plan has to match their clock.
