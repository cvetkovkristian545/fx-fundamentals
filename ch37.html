●40.2 Security: wallets and the seed phrase

In crypto there is no bank to call if you make a mistake. That is why security is your responsibility from day one.

Where you hold itWho holds the keysPlusMinus
Centralised exchange (CEX)The exchangeEasy buying and selling in eurosIf the exchange goes bust or is hacked, your money may disappear (FTX, 2022)
Software walletYouFull control, freeThe phone or computer can be infected
Hardware walletYou, offlineThe safest option for larger sumsCosts money; needs care during setup
  1. The seed phrase (12 or 24 words) is the wallet itself. Whoever has it has the money.
  2. Write it on paper or metal. Never a photo, never in the cloud, never in an email or chat.
  3. No legitimate person will ever ask you for your seed phrase. Not "support", not "the admin".
  4. Test with a small amount before moving a large one. Check the address character by character.
  5. Two-factor protection with an app, not SMS, on every exchange.
Myth

"A big exchange is like a bank, my money is guaranteed there."

Reality

Crypto on an exchange is not a bank deposit and is not covered by the 100,000 euro guarantee. MiCA improves the rules for safeguarding client assets, but does not turn them into a deposit.

Seed phrase · seed фраза12 or 24 words from which the wallet is restored.
CEX / DEXA centralised exchange (a company) versus a decentralised one (a smart contract, no intermediary).
Self-custody · самопопечителствоYou hold the keys, not a third party.

●●40.3 DeFi, CEX and DEX

DeFi (decentralised finance) are blockchain applications that do what banks and exchanges do, but with no company in the middle: swaps, loans, yield. The plus is freedom. The minus is that a bug in the code, a scam project or a wrongly pressed button has nobody to fix it.

CEX

A company, registration with documents, support, euros by bank transfer. Risk from the company itself.

DEX

A smart contract, you connect your wallet, no registration. Risk from the code and from scam tokens.

Yield

If something promises 20% a year in stablecoins, ask where it comes from. If you can't answer, the yield is you.

Beginner

A regulated CEX for buying, a hardware wallet for holding, DeFi later and with small sums.

Smart contract · смарт договорA program on the blockchain that executes itself when conditions are met.
Rug pullA scam in which a project's creators withdraw the money and vanish.

●●40.4 Regulation in the EU: MiCA and DAC8

RuleSince whenWhat it means for you
MiCAFully applicable from 30 December 2024, with a transition period for providers until mid-2026Crypto exchanges in the EU must be licensed, keep assets segregated and follow rules for stablecoins.
DAC8From 1 January 2026Exchanges collect data on the trades of EU clients and report it to the tax authorities. The data for 2026 is exchanged between countries by 30 September 2027.

The practical takeaway: choose an exchange licensed under MiCA and keep your own records. Taxes are in Chapter 41.

Beyond the EU

It is worth following the US crypto market structure law (the Clarity Act) too. In September 2026 it failed a procedural vote in the Senate, and the market reacted weakly because the failure was expected. The lesson is familiar from Chapter 29: an expected event rarely moves price much.

MiCAMarkets in Crypto-Assets: the EU regulation on crypto-assets.
CASPCrypto-asset service provider: the licensed exchange or wallet.
DAC8The EU directive on exchanging tax information about crypto-assets.

●●●40.5 Crypto through macro glasses

Bitcoin is often advertised as "digital gold", but most of the time it behaves like a risk asset, highly sensitive to liquidity. That is why everything from Part III applies.

More liquidity (QE, lower rates)SupportCentral bank balance sheets

Fear and greed indexExtremes are contrarian signalsCoinMarketCap
BTC dominanceAbove 60%: money stays in BTC. A drop toward 50–55%: rotation into altcoins.TradingView: BTC.D

What is specific to crypto is leverage. Crypto exchanges trade perpetual futures with high leverage. When many leveraged positions are stacked at one level, a move to it triggers forced closures (liquidations), which push price further. The liquidation map in CoinGlass shows where these clusters are. It is the same logic as liquidity and stops from Chapter 7, only more visible.

Mentor's note

On the morning of 21 October 2026 bitcoin is around 82,600 dollars, and below ether (ETH) there are about 1 billion dollars of long positions that would be liquidated below 2,333. The numbers will be stale when you read this. The idea is not: a liquidation cluster is a magnet and fuel at the same time.

Crypto YouTube channels also follow longer signals: the 50-week moving average as the border between a bullish and a bearish regime (a weekly close above it after a long stay below often confirms the bottom is in) and the idea that a real "altseason" comes after a new bitcoin high and a drop in its dominance. Both are useful as context, not as an entry signal.

Perpetual futures · перпетюъл фючърсиFutures with no expiry, the main leverage instrument in crypto.
Liquidation · ликвидацияThe forced closure of a leveraged position when the margin runs out.
Funding rateA periodic payment between longs and shorts in futures. A high positive funding means crowded longs.
Debrief

What you take from Dossier 40

  • BTC: 21 million, a halving about every 4 years. Altcoins are riskier. Stablecoins are the bridge to the dollar.
  • The seed phrase is the wallet. Never a photo, never to anyone.
  • Crypto on an exchange is not a bank deposit.
  • MiCA licenses exchanges; DAC8 reports your data to the tax authorities from 2026.
  • Real yields, the dollar, liquidity and ETF flows move crypto. Liquidation maps show the fuel.

The US 10y yield is rising for a third day, DXY is above a key level, and bitcoin ETF flows have been negative two days in a row. Bitcoin is 1.5% above a big cluster of long liquidations, and the funding rate is high and positive. A friend messages you: "Exchange support wrote to me that I have to verify my wallet with my seed phrase. Should I do it?"

Task: what do the macro signals say about bitcoin? What do the liquidation cluster and funding mean? What do you reply to your friend?

Sample answer
  1. Rising yields, a strong dollar and ETF outflows are three headwinds against bitcoin. The picture is mostly bearish for the short horizon, without being certain.
  2. A high positive funding means crowded long positions. The liquidation cluster 1.5% below is a magnet: a move to it can trigger a cascade of forced selling. A bounce often follows such a cascade, because the fuel has burned. This is a scenario, not a forecast.
  3. To the friend: "No. It's a scam. No real support asks for a seed phrase. Don't reply, don't click links, change your passwords and move the funds if you shared anything." There are no probabilities here.
