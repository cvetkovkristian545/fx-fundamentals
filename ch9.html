Sessions, Kill Zones and Volatility

There is a time to go long, a time to go short and a time to go fishing. Jesse Livermore (attributed)

After this chapter you will be able to

  • tell the story of a typical day: Asia builds the range, London sweeps it, New York delivers the move;
  • name the kill zones in Sofia time and know when not to open anything new;
  • use ATR to judge how far away the stop should be.

●9.1 A day in three acts

Every trading day has a recurring rhythm that shows more clearly in currencies than anywhere else. It doesn't happen every day, but it happens often enough to be the basis of the daily plan.

Mentor's note

Asia sets the stage, London runs the false plot, New York reveals the killer. Don't walk out of the cinema during the first act.

Asia range · азиатски диапазонThe high and low of the overnight session. A target for London.
Expansion · разширениеThe big move of the day, usually after a sweep.
Judas swingThe false initial move against the day's direction.

●9.2 Kill zones in Sofia time

Kill zones are the short windows when the biggest volume and the cleanest moves arrive. Outside them the market either sleeps or wanders without direction. The good news: in Sofia time they keep the same hours in summer and winter.

During the few weeks in March and October–November when the US and Europe change their clocks on different dates, the New York windows come one hour earlier in Sofia time.

●●9.4 ATR: how far away the stop should be

If your stop is smaller than the usual noise of the candles, a random wick will hit it even when the analysis is right. The simple rule in this book: the minimum stop is half the ATR(14) of the timeframe you make the decision on, plus a buffer of a few pips for wicks and spread. If the M5 ATR is too small, use the M15 ATR.

SituationAction
News, a move over 2× ATRWait for it to calm down; don't enter the spike.
Normal volatilityStandard size.
Very low volatility (under 0.5× ATR)Expect a breakout, but wait for a signal.
Asian rangeWatch, don't trade (except JPY).
Myth

A tight stop is a safe stop.

Reality

A tight stop is a frequent stop. Safety comes from position size (Chapter 11), not from how close the stop is.

Buffer · буферExtra pips beyond the level to survive a random wick and the spread.

●●●Go deeperFixes and month-end flows▾

Every day at 16:00 London time (18:00 Sofia) the WM/Reuters fix sets the benchmark rates used to value trillions in funds and indices. Many large orders are executed "at the fix", inside a five-minute window around it. In Tokyo there is a similar fixing at 09:55 local time (02:55 Sofia).

On the last business days of the month, global funds rebalance: if foreign stocks rose a lot, they sell some and convert the money back, which creates currency flows unrelated to any news. Banks publish estimates of these flows in the last week of the month.

For a scalper: sharp, newsless moves between 17:55 and 18:05 Sofia, especially at month-end, are usually fix flows. They often reverse afterwards. Do not read them as a new trend, and do not put a tight stop next to price at that time.

Debrief

What you take from Dossier 09

  • Asia builds a range, London sweeps it, New York delivers the move.
  • Kill zones (Sofia): London 10:00–12:00, New York 15:30–18:00. No new positions after 18:00.
  • Every pair has its own character and ATR. Check the ATR before you trade.
  • Minimum stop ≈ 0.5 × the timeframe's ATR plus a buffer.
  • On a news spike over 2× ATR: you wait.

No single right answer

Tuesday. Asia on EURUSD: 1.0832–1.0848 (16 pips). The daily ATR is 70 pips. At 10:20 London breaks up to 1.0855 and comes back below 1.0848. D1 is bearish. There are US retail sales at 15:30 (orange folder). It is 11:05.

Task: what happened in London? What does ATR say about the rest of the day? Which is the next window where you expect a move, and what will you watch?

Sample answer
  1. London swept the Asian high (BSL above 1.0848) and came back inside: a sweep against the bearish D1. A classic false first act.
  2. About 23 pips of 70 have been covered. There is room for about 40–50 pips of movement, probably down towards the SSL below 1.0832 and further.
  3. If there is an MSS down on M5/M15 after the sweep, the zone it started from is worth watching while still in the London window (until 12:00).
  4. The next window is New York after 15:30. Retail sales are medium importance: a buffer of about 15–30 minutes, then I look for continuation down towards the liquidity under Asia.
